## Best Representation of Data

- Mean of about 5. Indicates that the average SOT of mobile users is 5 hours.
- This represents the majority of the population.
- Outliers such as min or max do not represent the entire population.
- Mode is very misleading for numerical data of such variation.
- Median as well could be a good indicator of a common middle ground, but the mean is the best representation.

## Our Data
